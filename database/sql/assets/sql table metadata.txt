finance.assets — common asset identity
Every searchable stock, cryptocurrency, or commodity gets exactly one asset record.
Column	Purpose
id	UUID primary key
symbol	Ticker or asset symbol, e.g. AAPL, BTC, GOLD
name	Full asset name
asset_type	stock, crypto, or commodity
description	General description
category	Broad classification, e.g. technology, DeFi, energy
logo_url	Logo or icon
website_url	Official website
external_ids	JSONB mapping to provider IDs, if needed
created_at	Record creation time
updated_at	Last profile update

Important: symbols aren't globally unique. Different assets can share a ticker, and the same stock can trade on multiple exchanges. Search and identity resolution should use exchange, network, provider ID, or contract address where applicable—not just the symbol.











finance.stocks — stock-specific profile
One row per stock, linked by asset_id.
Column	Purpose
asset_id	Primary key and foreign key to assets.id
exchange	Primary listing exchange
sector	Broad business sector
industry	Specific industry
business_summary	What the company does
financial_statements	JSONB for annual and quarterly income statements, balance sheets, and cash-flow statements
financial_metrics	JSONB for revenue growth, earnings, margins, valuation ratios, debt, dividends, and related metrics

















finance.crypto — cryptocurrency-specific profile
Column	Purpose
asset_id	Primary key and foreign key to assets.id
network	Primary blockchain/network
contract_addresses	JSONB for contract addresses by network
circulating_supply	Currently circulating token supply
total_supply	Total issued supply, where available
max_supply	Maximum supply, if defined
tokenomics	JSONB for emissions, unlocks, allocations, burns, and vesting
holder_distribution	JSONB for holder concentration and distribution metrics
contract_analysis	JSONB for contract features, permissions, and security findings
socials	JSONB for official social/community links
exchanges	JSONB for known trading venues and relevant market pairs
security_summary	Concise security overview

Supply and holder information should include timestamps and sources because providers may report different values or update at different intervals.




















finance.commodities — commodity-specific profile
Column	Purpose
asset_id	Primary key and foreign key to assets.id
commodity_type	Energy, precious metal, industrial metal, agriculture, etc.
benchmark	Relevant benchmark or traded instrument
currency	Currency used for quoted prices
unit_of_measure	Such as barrels, troy ounces, or bushels
uses	Major industrial or commercial uses
value_drivers	JSONB for important supply, demand, and pricing factors
supply_demand	JSONB for production, inventories, consumption, and related data
inflation_data	JSONB for relevant inflation and price-index context

A commodity's benchmark matters: the price of one benchmark for crude oil, for example, is not automatically interchangeable with another.




















finance.information — research and changing intelligence
This is the major change. Rather than having a single large JSONB object per asset that gets overwritten, I'd make this a collection of information records.
Column	Purpose
id	UUID primary key
asset_id	Foreign key to assets.id
information_type	Type of information stored
data	JSONB containing the structured information
source	Provider, publisher, or originating source
source_url	Original article or source URL, where applicable
published_at	When the source information was published
observed_at	When the event or measurement occurred, if applicable
collected_at	When our platform retrieved it

The information_type could identify records such as:
- news — individual news articles and their summaries.
- event — earnings, regulatory decisions, product launches, exploits, and other material events.
- sentiment — sentiment scores, methodology, sample size, and the period analyzed.
- market_snapshot — current price, market capitalization, 24-hour volume, percentage changes, and available liquidity metrics.
- market_analysis — structured findings about market conditions.
- macro_context — relevant interest rates, inflation releases, economic conditions, and other external drivers.













finance.asset_price_history — historical price series
This remains a separate table because it can grow far faster than the profile tables.
Column	Purpose
id	Primary key
asset_id	Foreign key to assets.id
timestamp	Start time of the price interval
interval	For example, 1h or 1d
open	Opening price
high	Highest price
low	Lowest price
close	Closing price
volume	Traded volume for the interval

Use a uniqueness constraint on (asset_id, interval, timestamp) so repeated ingestion updates the same candle instead of creating duplicates.

One caveat: volume needs a documented unit. For stocks, it commonly represents shares traded; for crypto, token quantity or quote-currency volume depends on the feed; and for commodity futures, it may represent contracts traded. Normalize the meaning and unit, or store them explicitly when they differ.
